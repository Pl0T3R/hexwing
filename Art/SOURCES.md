# Art sources

Every model and texture in `Assets/HexWing/Resources/Models` and `Resources/Textures` is generated by the Blender scripts in `Art/blender` (original designs, procedural materials baked to textures). No third-party files are used yet.

When a CC0 asset is added (ambientCG, Poly Haven, Kenney, Quaternius), list it here: file, source URL, author, licence (CC0 1.0), and what it is used for.
